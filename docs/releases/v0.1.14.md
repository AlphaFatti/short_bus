This preview retunes 5-Band Assist to preserve the mix's clarity.

- Bass and low-mid cuts are gentler. The upper three bands react to brief rises above the current mix balance, with smaller cut limits and faster recovery.
- **Keep Manual** lets you run Assist after your manual 5-Band settings, including any lift. Turn **Focus** to 0% to hear the manual processing by itself. Existing presets and sessions load with Keep Manual off.

### Install

| Platform | ZIP | Place `Short Bus.vst3` in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Apple Silicon Mac | `Short-Bus-v0.1.14-macOS-arm64.zip` | `~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/` |
| Windows x64 | `Short-Bus-v0.1.14-Windows-x64.zip` | `C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\` |

Quit your audio host and replace the entire VST3 bundle. Rescan in REAPER or your VST3 host. In OBS on Windows, load it through atkAudio PluginHost or PluginHost2. The Mac build is ad hoc signed and unnotarized.
