This preview adds 5-Band Assist, stage resets, and a more detailed RTA.

- Enable 5-Band, turn on **Assist**, and choose **Speech** or **Music**. **Focus** adjusts the strength of its adaptive cuts. The display shows the gain change for each band as you listen. Turn Assist off to return to manual control.
- Use **Reset** in any stage header to confirm a return to factory settings. The RTA reset also clears the captured reference.
- The **RTA** shows tap RMS and peak, band levels in dBFS, and numeric comparisons against a reference. Hover over the Fine plot for a frequency range and readout. **Pink Slope** compares the signal with level-matched pink noise in both Fine and Broad.

### Install

| Platform | ZIP | Place `Short Bus.vst3` in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Apple Silicon Mac | `Short-Bus-v0.1.13-macOS-arm64.zip` | `~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/` |
| Windows x64 | `Short-Bus-v0.1.13-Windows-x64.zip` | `C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\` |

Extract the ZIP and copy the entire bundle into the folder. Rescan in REAPER or your VST3 host. In OBS on Windows, load it through atkAudio PluginHost or PluginHost2. The Mac build is ad hoc signed and unnotarized.

Assist is ready for feedback on service recordings. Include the source material, profile, Focus, and which bands showed cuts. [Report feedback](https://github.com/AlphaFatti/short_bus/issues/new)
