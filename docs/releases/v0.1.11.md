Short Bus is an open-source VST3 mastering rack with expansion and lift, five-band compression, three bus compressor modes, a 31-band GEQ with saturation, and a lookahead limiter. This preview adds a handle to the preset door and includes live transfer dots, scrolling meter history, and a movable RTA.

### Install the preview

| Platform | ZIP | Place `Short Bus.vst3` in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Apple Silicon Mac | `Short-Bus-v0.1.11-macOS-arm64.zip` | `~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/` |
| Windows x64 | `Short-Bus-v0.1.11-Windows-x64.zip` | `C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\` |

Extract the ZIP and copy the entire `Short Bus.vst3` bundle into the indicated folder. Rescan in REAPER or another VST3 host. In OBS on Windows, load it through atkAudio PluginHost or PluginHost2. [Setup and controls](https://github.com/AlphaFatti/short_bus/blob/v0.1.11/README.md)

The Mac preview is ad hoc signed and unnotarized. macOS may ask you to approve the downloaded plug-in before your host loads it.

### Try it and send feedback

Play a mix, turn on a stage, and try dragging its tile to change the order. Check that meters follow the audio and that your settings recall after reopening your session. For feedback, include your OS, host and version, and the steps that reproduce the behavior. [Report an issue](https://github.com/AlphaFatti/short_bus/issues/new)
